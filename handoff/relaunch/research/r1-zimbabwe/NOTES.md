# R1 Zimbabwe: research notes

Route: Victoria Falls → Hwange → Bulawayo → Matobo, 11 nights as drawn (variants 8–13).
Researched 2026-11-09. Sources are in `route.json`, per stay and at route level. Anything without a source there is marked *unverified*.

## Shape of the route and why

| Nights | Where | Primary | Alternatives |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Victoria Falls | Victoria Falls Safari Lodge (B&B from $288) | Ilala Lodge ($271 B&B), The Elephant Camp ($835+ FI, above band) |
| 3–4 | Hwange, eastern edge near the A8 | Ivory Lodge (FI from $494, park fees included) | Hwange Safari Lodge (on the road, $318 FB) |
| 5–7 | Hwange, eastern boundary | The Hide (FI from $467, 4×4 drive-in) | Davison's (park-and-transfer, $615–1,010), Camelthorn (park-and-transfer via Halfway House, $500, *access unverified*) |
| 8 | Bulawayo | The Bulawayo Club (on the road, ~US$89–100/room) | Travellers' Guest House (on the road, rate unverified) |
| 9–11 | Matobo Hills | Amalinda Lodge (FI from $411) | Big Cave Camp ($325–400), Farmhouse Lodge ($124) |
| 12 | Out via BUQ, or back to the Falls (2 more days, 1 night in Hwange) | | |

- **Two Hwange stays, not one.** Ivory Lodge is drive-in just off the A8 with park fees and guided drives in the rate, so day one in Hwange needs no gate logistics. The Hide is the deeper, wilder second half, and one of the few camps on the eastern side that states in writing that self-drivers may drive in (4WD). Three nights there also unlocks the free Dove's Nest sleep-out and the free Main Camp transfer, which is the fallback if the traveller doesn't want to drive the dirt.
- **Bulawayo night is deliberate.** Hwange to Bulawayo is the hardest day (roughly 340 km, most of it on the damaged A8). Driving straight on to Matobo risks arriving after dark. Europcar's own advisory forbids night driving on the A8.
- **Matobo gets three nights.** Rhino tracking, rock art and World's View need two full days, and three nights puts a rest after the A8.
- **Ending in Bulawayo vs looping back.** Ending at BUQ (nonstop flights to Johannesburg, per flight aggregators) is shorter but needs a one-way rental, which Europcar actively discourages on this road. Looping back adds about 485 km and a night in Hwange. I've drawn the Bulawayo ending as primary with the loop as a variant. **Dom to choose** (see decisions).

## Roads

- **A8 Victoria Falls–Hwange–Bulawayo (≈440 km):** badly damaged; rehabilitation under a PPP with eight contractors began mid-2025, stalled, and was due to remobilise in August–September 2026 (CITE, 13 Aug 2026; minister said ~57 km done). Overloaded coal trucks are reported to be destroying repaired sections. Europcar Zimbabwe: "deteriorated considerably", unsuitable for sedans, 20–30 km/h for much of it, 8–9 hours end to end, no night driving. Older reports name Halfway–Gwayi, Lupane–Cross Jotsholo and Insuza–Lupane as worst. **Conditions change month to month. Check before every departure; the road book should carry a "last checked" date.**
- **Main Camp turn-off (Cross Mabale):** 172 km from Victoria Falls, 283 km from Bulawayo; 23 km tar to Main Camp.
- **Matobo access:** ZimParks: tar from Bulawayo (Robert Mugabe Way → Matopos Road), two-lane for ~30 km to the boundary, then single-lane tar to Maleme Dam. Internal roads gravel, "suitable for most vehicles"; some areas need high-clearance 4WD (Whovi; Toghwana in the rains).
- **Drive times:** day 3 (2.5–4.5 h) and day 8 (6–8 h) are ranges because the sources disagree and the road is changing. Day 8 is my estimate, scaled from Europcar's figure, and is marked unverified.

## Stays considered and rejected

- **Gorges Lodge:** closed for renovation "until further notice" (Victoria Falls Guide).
- **Matetsi, Victoria Falls River Lodge:** in our listings already, but no self-drive access fact found. River Lodge is in Zambezi NP. Matetsi is likely above band. Not needed with three Falls options. Can be added if Dom wants a river lodge.
- **Linkwasha, Little Makalolo, Somalisa, Camp Hwange:** price and/or access (above).
- **Miombo Safari Camp:** drive-in on tar near Main Camp and marketed to self-drivers, which is promising. But the only rate I found was a tour operator's package figure ($510–560), and room counts conflict. Worth a direct rate request; could replace Hwange Safari Lodge as the mid-price Hwange option.
- **ZimParks chalets (Main Camp, Maleme):** self-catering, no guided activities included. They fail criterion 3 as a primary but are worth a line in the road book.
- **Matobo Hills Lodge:** no reliable information found beyond booking-site listings.

## Gaps and things I could not verify

- **Official 2026 ZimParks tariff:** the PDF was too large to fetch. Park fee figures are secondary: $12/day Hwange international overnight; $10/$3 vehicle; Matobo per-person unknown. Someone should open the PDF and confirm before anything is published.
- **The Hide's own rack rate:** in a PDF I couldn't read. $467 low season is from Victoria Falls Guide.
- **Ilala Lodge:** only the rate is sourced. Everything else is a placeholder. Needs a proper check, or drop it.
- **Travellers' Guest House:** one first-hand source, no rate.
- **The Bulawayo Club:** history and founding date not sourced. Don't publish historical claims yet. No per-person rate (room rates only, from booking sites).
- **Hwange Safari Lodge:** owner unverified. Kept only as an on-the-road lunch and fallback bed. It's a ~200-guest hotel and doesn't pass the friend test as a main stay.
- **Halfway House:** whether it sells fuel and food today is unverified.
- **Painted Dog Conservation centre:** hours and fee conflict (free entry and 8–5 per visitor reports). Email ahead.
- **Gate times:** 06:00–18:00 at Hwange from secondary listings. Matobo's single source is weak.
- **Lat/lng:** only The Hide (operator access page) and The Bulawayo Club (Expert Africa owner page) have sourced coordinates. The rest are null.
- **Roadblock kit:** what's legally required for rental vehicles is inconsistent across sources. Warning triangles are the recurring fine. The road book should say: check the kit at handover.
