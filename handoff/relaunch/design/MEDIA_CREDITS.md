# Media credits — self-drive relaunch

**Compiled:** 2026-11-09. **Manifest:** `lib/relaunch/media.ts` (same data, typed). **Files:** `public/media/<area>/`.

## How to use these credits

- **Wikimedia CC BY / CC BY-SA files must carry their credit line** wherever the image appears (caption, hover or a credits page linked from the page). The ready-to-print string is in the `credit` field. CC BY-SA images were resized and converted to WebP; the credit line says so.
- **Pexels files** (Pexels licence) need no attribution. Crediting the photographer is optional and a courtesy.
- **CC0 / public domain** files need no credit.
- `verifiedPlace: false` means the image is real but we could not confirm the exact place. Caption it generically, never with a specific lodge or park name.
- All photos had EXIF and GPS metadata stripped by sharp. This matters for the rhino images (Matobo, Okaukuejo). Consider captioning rhino images by region only.
- Photos: WebP, max 2400 px long edge, quality 78, plus a `-1200.webp` card version. Videos: Pexels 1280×720 MP4 renditions with a WebP poster taken from the Pexels thumbnail.
