## 1. How to shoot every frame

### Orientation and crops

Every frame ends up in more than one shape. Shoot so it survives all of them:

| Use | Shape | What it means when you shoot |
|---|---|---|
| Site heroes and route pages | **16:9** landscape | Shoot landscape. Leave sky or ground above and below the subject: the 3:2 frame loses a strip top and bottom at 16:9 |
| Instagram feed | **4:5** portrait | Either shoot it vertically, or keep the subject well inside a landscape frame so a 4:5 crop can be taken from the middle |
| Phones (site heroes crop to portrait on mobile) | **Portrait safe zone** | Keep the subject, and anything that must be seen, inside the **centre 40% of the width** of a landscape frame. Edges get cut on a phone |

**For the priority A shots, take both:** one landscape frame composed for 16:9 with the subject central, and one vertical frame for Instagram. It's ten extra seconds and saves a bad crop later.

### Light

- **The first and last hour of sun.** Warm, low, real shadows. Midday light flattens everything and reads as a holiday snap.
- **November and December are the start of the rains** across Zimbabwe and northern Botswana (general seasonal pattern; check the forecast locally). Expect green, big skies and storms, less dust. That's honest and usable. Don't fake the dry-season look; the captions will say where and when.
- **Victoria Falls is near its lowest flow at this time of year.** A full-spray Falls shot (R1-01) won't happen until roughly March to May. Shoot the gorge and the bridge now; leave the "Falls in full flow" hero to the stock image.

### What to avoid, every time

- **Rooftop tents, recovery boards, camping kit, kitted campers.** It's exactly the audience we're steering away from. A plain 4×4 or SUV only.
- **Number plates.** Angle the vehicle so the plate isn't legible, or blur it before upload.
- **Faces without consent.** Hands, backs and silhouettes are fine. If anyone recognisable is in frame, get their permission (a written note or a short recorded "yes" on your phone) and log it. No one looking at camera, no selfie poses.
- **Rental company and partner branding.** We don't name partners (STRATEGY §5), so keep logos on the vehicle, key tags, paperwork and office signage out of frame.
- **Border posts.** Many prohibit photography outright, and officials take it seriously. Never shoot at the post, the queue, officials or the buildings. If you want the border feeling, shoot the road approaching it or the Kazungula bridge from a public spot well away from the post, and only where it's clearly allowed. When in doubt, don't.
- **Police, military and government buildings.** Don't photograph them, including roadblocks.
- **Drones** in or near national parks, the Falls or border areas need permits and are often banned outright. No drone shots unless you have a written permit.
- **Wildlife:** no baiting, no calls, no leaving the vehicle where it's not allowed, no flash at night.
- **Rock art:** no flash, no touching, no wetting. Check the site allows photography first.
- **Lodges:** you can photograph a lodge you stay at for your own use, but **don't publish it until the lodge has said yes** (see `marketing/LODGE_PHOTO_REQUEST.md`). Other guests out of frame.
- **Linkwasha and Somalisa** have left the collection (D-44). Don't shoot them for us.

### Rhino images: strip the location, always

Poaching networks use photo metadata. **Before any rhino image or clip leaves your camera or phone, the GPS location must be stripped.** This applies to the site, Instagram, LinkedIn, email and anything sent to a lodge or a designer.

- **iPhone:** in Photos, tap Share → Options → turn **Location** off before sending. Or Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Camera → Never, for the whole trip in rhino areas.
- **Mac:** in Photos, Image → Location → **Hide Location** before exporting, and untick "Include location information" in the export dialog.
- **Any file, any camera:** `exiftool -gps:all= -overwrite_original FILE.jpg` (works on videos too: `.mov`, `.mp4`). Then check with `exiftool -gps:all FILE.jpg`; it should print nothing.
- **Videos carry location as well.** Strip clips the same way.
- **Captions:** name the park at most ("Matobo National Park"), never a specific area, waterhole or time ("this morning at …"). Post rhino images days or weeks after taking them, not the same day.
- Don't rely on the site's image pipeline or Instagram to strip metadata for you.

Stripping GPS from everything else is good practice too, but for rhino it's non-negotiable.
