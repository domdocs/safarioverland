## Method

- **Accommodation only, per person sharing.** Vehicle, fuel, park and conservation fees, levies, border charges, activities not in a lodge's rate, transfers and flights are excluded.
- **Nights costed** are the itinerary as shown on the route page, including the optional Divundu nights on the four corners (the page says that without them the same two nights are spent at Nkasa Rupara, so the night count doesn't change).
- **Per night**, the cheapest stay offered for that night (primary or alternative) that shows a sourced rate on the route page. Stays the editorial layer marks "rate on request" (`route-editorial.ts`) are left out even where the research holds a figure, so the price never rests on a rate the page doesn't show.
- **No sourced option for a night:** a labelled assumption, set out below.
- **Rate basis:** every rate used is per person sharing per night as the research records it. The one per-room figure (Bulawayo Club) is divided by two. Grootberg's basis is unclear, so the higher reading is used (see Namibia).
- **Meal basis is not adjusted.** It varies from bed and breakfast to fully inclusive, and is listed per night.
- **Season:** the research's lowest published "from" figure, usually green or low season. Peak-season rates (roughly July to October) are higher at most stays; the per-night notes give the figures where the research has them.
- **Total rounded up** to the nearest US$50. The primary-stays figure is shown unrounded and rounded, for information only.

## Caveat

These figures are indicative "from" prices, built from the lowest published or agent rate we found for each night, usually in the green or low season, at November 2026 exchange rates. They cover accommodation only, per person sharing, and mix meal bases from bed and breakfast to fully inclusive, so the cheapest version of a route is not the same holiday as the version with the primary stays. Two nights in total rest on stated assumptions, one route rests on a special offer, and several rates come from agents or secondary listings rather than the lodges themselves. The vehicle, fuel, park fees and flights are priced separately with each traveller, and nothing here is a quote until the nights are held.
