Namibia, the long way round.

Windhoek, the Kalahari, the dunes at Sossusvlei, the cold coast at Swakopmund, Damaraland, and Etosha, then back to Windhoek. Fourteen to sixteen nights, from US$2,800 per person sharing (lodges only), much of it on gravel, in Africa's best-known self-drive country.

We plan the days so you arrive before the light goes, and book places where someone will take you out on foot or by vehicle once you've parked. Our partners on the ground in Windhoek hand over the vehicle.
