Zimbabwe, from Victoria Falls.

Victoria Falls, then south and east to Hwange's waterholes, then on to the granite hills of Matobo, finishing in Bulawayo. About ten to twelve nights, from US$3,050 per person sharing (lodges only), and you can shorten it or join it to another route.

We start here because it's home ground. Our partners on the ground in Victoria Falls hand over the vehicle and are there if something goes wrong, and there's a line answered day and night while you drive.
