The drive is part of the safari.

We now plan and book self-drive journeys through southern Africa. You drive. Each night ends at a camp or lodge that gives you more than a bed: a guide who can read a track, a hide at a waterhole, a view worth the road.

Three routes to start. Zimbabwe from Victoria Falls. Namibia from Windhoek. And the four corners, where Zimbabwe, Botswana, Namibia and Zambia meet.
