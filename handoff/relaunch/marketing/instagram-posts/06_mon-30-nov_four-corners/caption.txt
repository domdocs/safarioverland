The four corners.

A short route that follows the water: Victoria Falls, the Chobe riverfront in Botswana, then west into Namibia's Zambezi Region and back. Eight to ten nights, from US$2,900 per person sharing (lodges only), four countries meeting on the river.

It works on its own, or as the end of something longer. You collect the vehicle in Kasane and hand it back there; our partners on the ground in Kasane look after both ends. We prepare the border paperwork.
